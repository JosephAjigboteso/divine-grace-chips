ABOUT US

Simple beginnings. Serious crunch.

Divine Grace Chips is a locally made snack brand focused on producing fresh, crunchy and hygienically prepared plantain and potato chips.

We started with a simple goal — to make cleaner, better-tasting chips you can confidently enjoy and share with family and friends. Our chips are made in small batches so every pack stays tasty and crispy.
